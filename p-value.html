Statistical assessment

Test multiple SV values against zero.

Enter 2 to 30 values to run a two-sided, one-sample Student's t-test with population mean = 0. The significance level is alpha = 0.01 (99% confidence).

Separate numbers with spaces, commas, or new lines. Example: 0.35, 0.28 0.41 0.32

Method and interpretation

The test uses the sample standard deviation and compares the sample mean with 0. The p-value is two-sided. A result below 0.01 is treated as statistically significant at the 99% level. This calculation assumes independent observations and an approximately normally distributed population; use scientific judgement when these assumptions are not appropriate.
